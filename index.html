    Help

    CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files both work the same way. A header row plus columns for date, description, category, type, and amount work best. Only date and amount are required; the rest are filled in with sensible defaults if missing.

    Dates can be day-first (31/01/2024) or month-first (01/31/2024): the app looks at your whole file to figure out which one you're using, so stay consistent within a single file. If there's no type column, a negative or positive amount decides whether a row counts as income or an expense.

    You'll stay right here on the Home page, with a quick preview: net position, income, expenses, and a side-by-side look at your first few rows (raw values next to how they were interpreted).

    If anything looks off (a date format, a stray column, a blank amount), it's listed right below that comparison and View full analysis stays disabled until you fix the file and re-upload. Once it's clean, that button takes you to the charts and full transaction table.

    At the top of the Analysis page, the Year and Month filters are built from whatever's actually in your data. Choosing one narrows the other down to combinations that exist.

    Whatever you pick reshapes everything below it: the net position, income and expenses, both time-based charts, and the transaction table.

    A small "Category:" pill appears next to the other filters whenever one's active, with a × to clear it. The donut charts themselves always show every category so you can keep adding or swapping selections without losing your place.

    Don't see your currency listed? Choose "Custom..." and type in a 3-letter code (like GHS or XAF) to use as the prefix instead.

    Click a column header to sort by it, click again to flip direction, and a third click clears that sort. Clicking another header while one is active adds it as a tiebreaker: small numbers on each header show the sort priority.
